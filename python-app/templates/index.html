🛡️ Zero-Account Privacy Architecture

• No User Database: Randomtalks runs completely in-memory. No databases, usernames, passwords, or personal logs exist.

• Peer-to-Peer Media: Voice and video streams are encrypted directly between browsers using WebRTC and never touch or record to a disk server.

• Ephemeral Identity: Closing your tab immediately terminates your random session token forever.
